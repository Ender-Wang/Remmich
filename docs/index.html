Your Readonly Immich Library Native on iOS/iPadOS

Browse, search, and revisit your photos and videos on iPhone and iPad. Remmich connects to your own Immich server and keeps your library read-only.

Add to SideStore Latest release

Requires iOS or iPadOS 26.6 or later

Explore Remmich

Photos

Browse your library by capture date and pinch to change the thumbnail layout.

Albums

Browse your own and shared albums, then return to the album you were viewing.

Library

Revisit Memories and favorites. Browse indexed collections and cached thumbnails offline.

Search

Describe a scene, find a filename, or search for text inside a photo. Narrow results with native filters.

Date cards

Scan groups of photos by date, with a cover and item count for every group.

Photo viewer

Zoom in for detail, use the filmstrip to browse nearby photos, or share and save the one you’re viewing.

Photo info

See when a photo was taken, which camera captured it, and details such as file size and dimensions.

Memories

Rediscover photos taken on this day in previous years.

Places

Explore your photos on a heatmap. Tap a place to open its timeline.

Map

See exactly where a geotagged photo was taken on a map below its details.

Profile

Switch accounts, manage server connections, and check your library indexes and offline storage.

Onboarding

Connect to your Immich server or leave the address blank to explore the demo.

Install Remmich

Connect to your Immich server after installing. To explore the demo, leave the server address empty at sign-in.

SideStore

With SideStore installed, add Remmich’s source on your device. You can also copy the source URL and paste it in SideStore under Sources → +.

Install from an IPA

Download the unsigned IPA from the latest release and sign it with SideStore, AltStore Classic, or another compatible sideloading tool.
